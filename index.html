Who I Am

I'm a game developer based in Toronto, ON, specializing in Unity (C#) and C++, with a passion for building gameplay systems that feel great to interact with.

From designing data-driven cutscene engines to crafting educational card games that make memory management fun, I love tackling the intersection of storytelling, systems design, and player experience.

My heart belongs to cozy RPGs and story-driven games — think Stardew Valley, Super Mario RPG, and Sailor Moon. That same love for games that teach and delight drives my work in educational game research at Humber Polytechnic.
